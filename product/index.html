Overview

Everything your business knows, in one place

Four windows into how Tayl works. The status shown on each stage comes from the product's feature configuration, not from this page.

01 · Connect

Your tools, read-only first

Google Workspace, Notion, Stripe and Slack connect through their own sign-in. Tayl reads the records you choose and keeps them current.

Available
02 · Context

Documents become part of the picture

Upload PDF, image, CSV, XLSX and DOCX files. Tayl reads them, links what it finds, and stores the understanding in its local database.

Available
03 · Intelligence

Ask in plain language

Questions are answered from your connected and uploaded data, with the sources attached. If the evidence is not there, the app should say so.

Available

Pipeline

From a connected tool to a decision you can defend

Each stage does a specific job. Some are available today; others are explicitly marked as coming soon.

01

Link the tools where your business already keeps its work.

Connect Google Workspace, Notion, Stripe and Slack through their own sign-in flows. Tayl reads the records you choose and keeps them current. Connectors start read-only.

For a small business owner: Stop tab-hopping between email, documents, invoices and chat just to work out what changed.

AvailableHow it fits together →
02

Turn raw files and connected records into business context.

Upload PDFs, images, CSV, XLSX and DOCX files. Tayl reads them, links what it finds to the rest of your business, and stores the result in its local database.

For a small business owner: Contracts, supplier invoices and exported reports stop being dead files you have to remember and search by hand.

AvailableHow it fits together →
03

Ask questions over your own data and see the evidence.

Ask a question in plain language. Tayl answers from the connected and uploaded data it has, and shows the sources behind the answer. When there is not enough evidence, it should say so.

For a small business owner: Get a straight answer about your own business without building a report or exporting a spreadsheet first.

AvailableHow it fits together →
04

See recommended next steps with the evidence attached.

Tayl turns what it understands into recommendations you can check. A recommendation is only as good as the evidence behind it, so the sources travel with it.

For a small business owner: Make a call with the relevant invoices, documents and messages in front of you instead of relying on memory.

AvailableHow it fits together →
05

Plan the steps an approved decision needs.

Once a decision is approved, Orchestration maps the steps required to carry it out across the tools you use.

For a small business owner: Spend less time working out who needs to do what after a decision is made.

Coming soonHow it fits together →
06

Carry approved actions into the tools you already use.

Execution will perform only the actions you explicitly approve, in the connected tools you have authorised. It is not available yet.

For a small business owner: Close the loop between deciding something and having it actually happen, without copy-pasting between tools.

Coming soonHow it fits together →
07

Keep the picture current as outcomes arrive.

Feedback will update the business context as the results of actions arrive, so the next recommendation starts from a fresher picture.

For a small business owner: Keep Tayl's understanding of the business from drifting out of date.

Coming soonHow it fits together →

Illustrative examples

What it looks like in a working week

These are generic examples of the kind of work Tayl is designed for, not customer stories or screenshots.

Illustrative example

A supplier invoice lands in your inbox

Upload the PDF and connect your workspace. Tayl reads the invoice, links it to the supplier and the relevant records, and keeps the evidence available when you ask about it.

Illustrative example

You need to know what changed this month

Ask a question in plain language. The answer is drawn from the tools and documents Tayl has, with the sources shown rather than a confident guess.

Illustrative example

A payment is late and you need a decision

Tayl can bring the invoice, the customer record and the related messages together, then show a recommendation with the evidence behind it.

Execution and write actions: Write actions are not available yet. Connectors are read-only while Execution is on the roadmap. Approved actions are not performed in your tools yet.
