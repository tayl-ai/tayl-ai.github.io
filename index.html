Mission

The system that understands your business should run on your machine.

Tayl is built around a simple position: your business data is yours, and the software that helps you understand it should not require you to hand that data to a hosted platform. The app runs locally on Windows, keeps its working data in a local SQLite database, and lets you choose the language model — cloud or local — that you trust with the questions you ask.

Our vision is a business operating system that earns its place by showing its work: answers with evidence, decisions with sources, and a clear line between what is known, what is recommended and what has actually been done.

Why Tayl

Built for owners who want their business back

Three commitments that shape the product, not three marketing adjectives.

Local-first by architecture

Business data lives on your Windows PC in a local SQLite database. There is no Tayl-hosted backend for user data.

Your keys, your choice

Choose a cloud LLM or a local LLM on first run and supply your own API keys. You decide where query content goes.

Evidence before confidence

Answers are grounded in the data Tayl has. Recommendations carry their sources, and the app should say when the evidence is not there.
