Connectors

What Tayl reads from each tool

The list below describes what Tayl reads. It does not imply write access, endorsement or a partnership.

Google Workspace

Read-only first
  • Google Docs and Sheets
  • Files in Google Drive
  • Document content you choose to connect

Connected through Google's own sign-in and authorisation flow. Read-only first.

Notion

Read-only first
  • Pages you have access to
  • Databases and their records
  • Page content that gives the business context

Connected through Notion's own sign-in and authorisation flow. Read-only first.

Stripe

Read-only first
  • Customer records
  • Invoices and their status
  • Payment context attached to invoices

Connected through Stripe's own sign-in and authorisation flow. Read-only first.

Slack

Read-only first
  • Channels you choose to connect
  • Messages in those channels
  • Conversation context around the work

Connected through Slack's own sign-in and authorisation flow. Read-only first.

Read-only first: Write actions are not available yet. Connectors read from your tools only while Execution is on the roadmap.
Your accounts

Your accounts, your terms

Each connected tool is your own account. Authorisation happens through that provider, and your use of the provider remains subject to its terms and privacy policy. Tayl does not create a separate hosted copy of your business data.

Local storage

Where the data goes

Connected records and uploaded documents are processed on your Windows PC and kept in Tayl's local SQLite database. There is no Tayl-hosted backend for user data. If you choose a cloud LLM, query content is sent to that provider.
